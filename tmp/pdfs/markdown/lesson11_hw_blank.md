1.

Researchers want to know whether drinking coffee affects reaction time in adults. They record

whether or not each participant drinks coffee daily (yes or no) and measure their reaction time

(in milliseconds)  using a computer test. Identify the variables in the study, and classify each as
categorical or quantitative.

2.

A researcher studies whether owning a pet is related to stress levels. She records whether or
not each adult owns a pet (yes/no), and their  score on a stress survey (0-50 scale). Sarah
identifies the variables as owning a pet and the score on the stress survey.

a. One of Sarah's variables is incorrectly identified. Which one? Correct her mistake.

b. Classify each variable as categorical or quantitative. Explain.

3.

In a recent study on walking and cardiovascular health, researchers analyzed data from 33,560
adults in the United Kingdom aged 40 to 79. Each participant wore a step-counter for one

week to measure how many steps they took, and if those steps were accumulated in long or

short walks. Researchers monitored these participants for 8 years, recording whether or not

the subject developed cardiovascular disease. They found that people whose walking was

concentrated in long walks had a lower incidence of cardiovascular disease, compared to those

whose walking was concentrated in short walks.

a. Identify the population and sample.

b. Is this a retrospective study or a prospective study? How do you know?

c. Identify and classify the variable(s)  in the study as categorical or quantitative.

d. Name at least one limitation for the study.

4.

Survey, which collects detailed 24-hour time diaries from a large sample of U.S. adults each

year. The study focused on workers in occupations with high exposure to generative Al  tools

like ChatGPT, comparing their time use before and after these tools became widely available.

Researchers found that workers in jobs with high Al exposure actually worked about 3 hours

more per week after the Al tools like ChatGPT became widely available.

a. Identify the population and sample.

b. Is this a retrospective study or a prospective study? How do you know?

c. Identify and classify the variable(s)  in the study.

5.

A college basketball coach wants to analyze performance for the players on her team during

the season. For the entire season, she records the total number of minutes played, points

scored, and rebounds for every player on the team across all games. Which of the following

best describes the data collection?

A) A sample, because only basketball players were included.

B) An experiment, because the coach is measuring player outcomes.

C) A census, because data were collected from every player in the team's population.

D) A retrospective study, because the coach is using historical player data.
